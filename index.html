FAQ

The fine print.

How long does shipping take? +
Standard US shipping is 3–5 business days. Express options available at checkout. International (Canada, UK, EU, Australia) varies by region — full ETAs shown at checkout.
What's the return policy? +
Free returns within 30 days on unworn items with tags. If anything's wrong on our end — wrong size, defect, misprint — we'll replace it free of charge.
What fabric and inseam? +
180gsm performance mesh polyester with 4-way stretch and moisture-wicking finish. Standard inseam is 9". Reinforced double-stitch seams throughout.
How does sizing run? +
True to size — XS to 3XL. Full measurements per size are on every product page. Between sizes, size up for a looser fit.
Will sold-out patterns come back? +
Most patterns are limited drops — once they sell out, they're often gone for good. Sign up for Drop Signal to get notified of restocks and new releases first.
Do you ship internationally? +
Yes — US, Canada, UK, EU, and Australia. Shipping cost and ETA show up at checkout based on your address.
