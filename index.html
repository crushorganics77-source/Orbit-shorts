FAQ

The fine print.

How long until my pair ships? +
Every pair is printed and stitched after you order. Production runs 3–5 business days, then 2–5 days US shipping. International varies by region.
What's the return policy? +
Free returns within 30 days on non-customized core styles. Custom designs are final sale — but if anything's wrong on our end, we remake it free.
Can I design my own pair? +
Yes. The Designer lets you customize colors per panel, add jersey number, drop in text, pick stripe colors. No minimum order.
What fabric and inseam? +
180gsm performance polyester with 4-way stretch and moisture-wicking finish. Standard inseam is 9".
How does sizing run? +
True to size — XS to 3XL. Full measurements per size are on every product page.
Do you ship internationally? +
Yes — US, Canada, UK, EU, Australia. Shipping cost and ETA show up at checkout.
