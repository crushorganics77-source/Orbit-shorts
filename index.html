FAQ

The fine print.

How long does shipping take? +
Standard US shipping is 3–5 business days. Express options available at checkout. International (Canada, UK, EU, Australia) varies by region.
What's the return policy? +
Free returns within 30 days on unworn items with tags. If anything's wrong on our end, we'll replace it free.
What fabric and inseam? +
180gsm performance mesh polyester with 4-way stretch and moisture-wicking finish. Standard inseam is 9".
How does sizing run? +
True to size — XS to 3XL. Full measurements per size on every product page. Between sizes, size up.
Will sold-out patterns come back? +
Most patterns are limited drops — once sold out, often gone for good. Sign up for Drop Signal for restocks.
Do you ship internationally? +
Yes — US, Canada, UK, EU, Australia. Cost and ETA show at checkout based on your address.
